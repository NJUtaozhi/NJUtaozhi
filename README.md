## About

I am an undergraduate student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Nanjing University.
My research interests lie in **multimodal affective computing**, **retrieval-augmented
generation (RAG)** and **LLM agents**.

I currently lead an early-stage research training project on an *Agentic RAG system for
multimodal mental-health analysis*, where I am responsible for the backend and the agent
workflow: a six-node LangGraph state machine over a multimodal (facial action unit + text
sentiment) pipeline, with retrieval-augmented generation and Dockerized microservices.
